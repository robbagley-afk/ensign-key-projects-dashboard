# Ensign Key Projects & Eisenhower Situational Awareness Dashboard

An executive dashboard for tracking and updating progress, maintaining situational awareness, and prioritizing key strategic initiatives for Ensign College Career Services and AI Teams.

## Strategic Alignment & Framework

- **Ensign College Mission**: *"Develop Capable and Trusted Disciples of Jesus Christ who are dedicated to strengthening Church, family, and community."*
- **Career Success Strategy**: *"Enable Career Success to help disciples become self-reliant with capability to serve and bless others in home, church, and community; and to stay on the covenant path."*
- **Eisenhower Matrix Prioritization**:
  - **Q1: Urgent & Important (Do First)**: Immediate critical path, blocker resolution, and high-impact deadlines.
  - **Q2: Important, Not Urgent (Strategic Mission Focus)**: Core student capability development, curriculum, mentoring network, and deep institutional foundations.
  - **Q3: Urgent, Not Important (Delegate & Automate)**: Repetitive bug tracking, operational triage, and automated pipelines.
  - **Q4: Not Urgent, Not Important (Triage & Deprioritize)**: Low-yield backlogs and legacy decommissioning.

## Features

- **2x2 Eisenhower Interactive Matrix**: Drag or re-assign projects across quadrants, view counts and urgent flags at a glance.
- **Detailed List & Table View**: Filter by Mission Pillar (Capable, Trusted, Strengthening Community) and Status (Active, Completed, Planned, On Hold).
- **Add / Edit Project Modal**: Update completion percentages, next milestones, lead owner, and target dates with persistent local browser state (`localStorage`).
- **Telemetry & Health**: Built-in Vercel Web Analytics and Speed Insights.
- **Design System Compliance**: Styled according to `cis-ui-ux-principles` (Montserrat & Inter typography, Ensign Deep Navy `#0A192F`, Forest Green `#004731`, WCAG AA accessibility, 56px touch targets).
